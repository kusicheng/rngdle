## Database, storage, and privacy policy
Data will be stored regarding your roll, roll time, connected email address, and password. We do not guarantee that these are encrypted and completely secure, please do not use sensitive emails or passwords.
Data will, in the future, be stored regarding any friends or connections you have. 

## Roll Math
The game will roll a random number between 0 and 1,000,000, inclusive. Points are calculated based on badges you get after rolling.

## Account management
You may create and delete an account at any time. Creating an account requires email confirmation, while deleting an account does not. Deleting an account does not delete your rolls, you only lose access to the account. There is no 2FA implemented for this website. Every account's rolls are public and this cannot be changed. No rolls can be deleted from an account.
